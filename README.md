# Hi, I'm Pablo 👋

📍 Miami, Florida · 🤖 AI engineer · 🎹 Classical pianist

I build applied AI systems at **Handtevy**, study computer science at the **University of Florida**, and play classical piano.

I'm also building **Accordo** — a platform to connect musicians with one another and with opportunities. Our ambition is to modernize the music community.

## What I'm building

- 🎼 **Accordo** — bringing my work as an engineer together with my life as a musician.
- 🎵 **[Gradus ad Parnassum](https://github.com/pablopupo/gradus-ad-parnassum)** — early experiments in retrieval over musical notation. Working toward answers grounded in specific measures of a score.
- 🩹 **[Kit AI](https://github.com/pablopupo/kit-ai)** — a team-built, offline-first first-aid PWA. My work includes IndexedDB retrieval and online/offline text-to-speech. A related fine-tuned Llama 3.2 model remains a separate experiment.
- 💸 **Nova** — Solana Pay invoicing, QR payments, and transaction tracking. Best Use of Solana at SwampHacks.
